Interpretation

The dashboard visualises modelled annual-equivalent labour-capacity and value-added exposure. Equivalent workers lost are full-time-equivalent productive capacity, not predicted layoffs. Value-added losses are first-order accounting quantities, not causal or general-equilibrium GDP forecasts.

The interface is an exploratory companion to the archived machine-readable outputs and does not introduce additional estimates.
