Interpretation

The dashboard visualises modelled annual-equivalent labour-capacity and value-added exposure. Equivalent workers lost are full-time-equivalent productive capacity, not predicted layoffs. Value-added losses are first-order accounting quantities, not causal or general-equilibrium GDP forecasts.

The sector explorer contains all 821 observed non-agricultural ATECO5 activities. Agriculture, forestry and fishing is retained as a separate branch-A aggregate, consistent with the core economic model, where agriculture is not forced into the ATECO5 mapping. In the main specification it is classified as heavy physical work with full outdoor exposure.

The agriculture rows shown here are reconciled from the whole-economy province totals and the complete non-agricultural ATECO5 microdata, so the “All branches” view reproduces the full-economy national totals rather than the non-agricultural subtotal.

Province exclusions are visual only. They affect the province map, ranking and visible table, but do not change the national KPI totals or the P1–P4 trajectory. This avoids turning a display choice into a change in the underlying national estimate.

Colour scaling. Adaptive mode clips the displayed colour range to the 5th–95th percentiles of visible province values to reduce domination by outliers. Visible-range mode uses the minimum and maximum among visible provinces. Fixed mode uses one common range across P1–P4 under the current sector/exposure filters to support direct temporal comparison.

The uncertainty view reports model-chain dispersion and methodological sensitivity. These ranges should not be interpreted as statistical confidence intervals.

The interface is an exploratory companion to the archived machine-readable outputs and does not introduce additional model estimates.
